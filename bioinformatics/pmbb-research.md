## In one paragraph

The Penn Medicine BioBank (PMBB) links genetic data to years of electronic health records for
hundreds of thousands of Penn Medicine patients. I use it extensively for biological research:
I design and run analyses that combine statistical genetics and machine learning to find the
genes and variants behind hearing loss, cardiovascular disease and breast cancer.

## Research areas

### Hearing loss

Rare-variant gene-burden tests and exome-wide association studies (ExWAS), in collaboration
between the Hall and Epstein labs. The work started as a replication of an earlier analysis and
grew into a pipeline designed to be rerun in other biobanks, so findings can be validated
independently. I present the results to the research groups.

### Cardiology

Machine-learning models trained on GPU clusters to study the genetic architecture of
cardiovascular traits, alongside association analyses.

## Methods

- **Statistical genetics:** gene-burden tests, ExWAS and GWAS, with multiple-testing correction
- **Machine learning** on HPC and GPU clusters
- **Scale:** billions of genetic variants and multi-terabyte datasets
- **Reproducibility:** pipelines built to be rerun in other biobanks
