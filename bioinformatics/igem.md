## The problem

Studying how genes and environmental exposures interact means combining genetic data with
exposure data that comes from many sources, in many formats. Researchers needed one place to
query those relationships instead of assembling them by hand for every study.

## What I built

- **A Python platform** with back end, front end, client and Docker deployment that integrates
  genetic and environmental-exposure data into a queryable knowledge base (GEdb).
- **A public website** (geneexposure.org) where researchers explore gene–exposure relationships.
- **A data integration system** for heterogeneous clinical-study datasets, with protection of
  personally identifiable information.
- **AI coding agents** brought into the development workflow on my own initiative.

## Impact

- GEdb is the knowledge base behind my first-author paper on environment-by-environment
  interaction studies ([PSB 2025](https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/proceedings/psb25/rico.pdf)).
