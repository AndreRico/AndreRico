**Role:** Business Intelligence Senior, then Senior Consultant · Klabin S/A, São Paulo · 2008 – 2021\

## The problem

Finance, HR and operations each needed reliable data for decisions, but that data lived in
different SAP modules and systems, with different rules.

## What I built

- **Data-warehouse solutions** for finance, HR and operations, with business rules in ABAP.
- **Managerial P&L automation** with ETL pipelines in Pentaho, Hive and Hadoop.
- **A predictive attrition model** on SAP HCM data that flagged employees at risk of leaving.
- **The SAP BW security model.**
- **Team leadership:** led BI project teams, with ownership of SLAs and data quality.

## Stack

SAP BW · ABAP · Pentaho · Hadoop · Hive
