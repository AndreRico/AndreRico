## How I got here

I started in IT with a degree in data processing, at the time when companies were first getting
connected. My first job was in **networks**: Cisco routers and Cisco certifications. Then came
**Windows networks**: installing and running Windows NT servers with SQL Server, Exchange and
Active Directory, both at ACSP.

At Cimpor I kept working in infrastructure as a contractor, but I wanted to join the company and
move closer to the data. So I taught myself **Visual Basic**, moved into development, and became an
**SAP analyst** just as SAP BW 3.5 arrived.

That BW experience opened the door to **Klabin** in 2008. I spent ten years building and running its
SAP BI platform, then built the **FP&A** area inside the data warehouse (2016 – 2018) and moved into
FP&A full time until 2021.

In the US, I joined **Penn State** in 2022 and kept a data focus while being pulled
into statistics, models and machine learning for biomedical research. Since 2024 I've been at the
**University of Pennsylvania**, building genomic data systems and doing research on the Penn
Medicine BioBank.

The domains changed, but the work stayed the same: take data that is spread out, build the system
that organizes it, and deliver something people trust to make decisions.
